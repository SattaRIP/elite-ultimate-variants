[h1]Elite & Ultimate Variants[/h1]

Add powerful elite and ultimate variants of creatures to Caves of Qud! Face challenging enhanced enemies with unique abilities, better loot, and devastating power.

[h2]Features at a Glance[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Elite Variants[/b] - Silver-glowing enhanced creatures with 2.0x-3.0x stat multipliers
[*][b]Ultimate Variants[/b] - Gold-glowing boss-tier enemies with 3.5x-6.0x stat multipliers
[*][b]Elite Armies[/b] - Coordinated groups of elites/ultimates with leader-goon dynamics
[*][b]5 Difficulty Presets[/b] - Easy (25%) to Nightmare (100%) with smart scaling
[*][b]Smart Preset System[/b] - Change difficulty mid-game seamlessly
[*][b]40+ Wish Commands[/b] - Complete control and testing capabilities
[/list]

[h2]Why This Mod is Different[/h2]

Unlike basic difficulty mods, Elite & Ultimate Variants provides:
[list]
[*]Mixed enhancement system (mutations + cybernetics + equipment)
[*]Natural spawning that respects zone tier and player level
[*]Balanced difficulty presets tested for all playstyles
[*]Army encounters with coordinated AI behavior
[*]Extensive customization without editing files
[/list]

[h2]🌟 Most Important Commands 🌟[/h2]

[b][code]elitepreset[/code] - Instant Preset Application[/b]
Press ` (backtick) → type [code]elitepreset[/code] → select difficulty
[list]
[*]Instantly applies one of 5 balanced presets
[*]Perfect for adjusting difficulty mid-game
[*]Shows confirmation when complete
[/list]

[b][code]eliteautopreset[/code] - Automatic Preset Changes[/b]
Press ` (backtick) → type [code]eliteautopreset[/code] → done!
[list]
[*]Enables automatic preset application from options menu
[*]Just change the dropdown and wait ~10 turns
[*]No more console commands needed!
[/list]

[i]These two commands make difficulty adjustment seamless - change presets on the fly without interrupting gameplay![/i]

[h2]Quick Start Guide[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Subscribe and enable the mod
[*]Start/load your game
[*]Press ` and type [code]eliteautopreset[/code]
[*]Open Options → Mods: Elite Variants → Select a preset
[*]Play! Elites will spawn when you reach level 16+ in tier 5+ zones (Normal preset)
[/olist]

[h2]Elite Army Types[/h2]

Five army compositions for varied challenges:

[b]Small Squad[/b] - 2-3 elite patrol (Level 15+, Tier 5+)
[b]Elite Patrol[/b] - 4-5 elite squad (Level 20+, Tier 6+)
[b]Elite Warband[/b] - 1 ultimate leader + 4-5 elite goons (Level 25+, Tier 7+)
[b]Ultimate Host[/b] - 2 ultimate leaders + 6-8 elite goons (Level 30+, Tier 8+)
[b]Titan Pair[/b] - 2-3 ultimates, no goons (Level 35+, Tier 8+)

[i]Maximum 1 army per zone to prevent overwhelming encounters[/i]

[h2]How Natural Spawning Works[/h2]

When a creature spawns, the mod checks:
[olist]
[*]Is natural spawning enabled?
[*]Is this creature eligible? (hostile, not merchant/vendor)
[*]Does zone tier meet minimum? (tier 5+ for Normal preset)
[*]Does player level meet minimum? (level 16+ for Normal preset)
[*]Zone density limit OK? (max elites per zone)
[*]Global cooldown expired? (prevents spawn spam)
[*]Random roll succeeds? (spawn chance %)
[/olist]

[b]Ultimate Priority:[/b] If player meets ultimate requirements, ultimate is tried first. At high levels/tiers, more ultimates spawn naturally!

[h2]All Wish Commands[/h2]

[b]Preset & Settings:[/b]
[code]elitepreset[/code] - Apply difficulty preset
[code]eliteautopreset[/code] - Enable auto-apply from options menu
[code]EliteVariantsSettings[/code] - Display current settings

[b]Spawning:[/b]
[code]spawnelite[/code] - Spawn elite at your location
[code]spawnultimate[/code] - Spawn ultimate at your location
[code]spawnarmy[/code] - Spawn elite army

[b]Natural Spawning Control:[/b]
[code]elitespawn:status[/code] - Show spawn statistics
[code]elitespawn:toggle[/code] - Toggle natural spawning
[code]elitespawn:reset[/code] - Reset spawn tracking

[b]Army System:[/b]
[code]elitearmy:status[/code] - Army statistics
[code]elitearmy:reset[/code] - Reset army tracking

[b]Debug:[/b]
[code]elitedebug:toggle[/code] - Toggle debug mode
[code]elitedebug:status[/code] - Debug status
[code]elitedebug:stats[/code] - Detailed spawn stats

[b]Utility:[/b]
[code]elitecleanup[/code] - Destroy all elites (fixes lag)

[i]Plus 20+ testing commands for developers![/i]

[h2]Legendary Creatures & Elite Variants[/h2]

Most vanilla legendaries are [b]excluded[/b] from natural transformation (they have exclusion tags).

However, you can force-spawn elite legendaries using [code]spawnelite[/code] or [code]spawnultimate[/code]:
[list]
[*]Preserves all original mutations and abilities
[*]Adds new mutations they don't already have
[*]Stacks elite bonuses on top of legendary power
[*]Creates [b]super-boss encounters[/b] far beyond normal difficulty
[/list]

[h2]Troubleshooting[/h2]

[b]Elites Not Spawning?[/b]
[list]
[*]Use [code]elitespawn:status[/code] to check if enabled
[*]Verify player level meets minimum (default: 16 for elites)
[*]Verify zone tier meets minimum (default: tier 5+)
[*]Use [code]elitedebug:stats[/code] to see failure reasons
[/list]

[b]Game Lag?[/b]
Use [code]elitecleanup[/code] to destroy all elites and clear tracking.

[b]Preset Not Applying?[/b]
[list]
[*]Wait ~10 turns if using options menu
[*]Or use [code]elitepreset[/code] for instant application
[*]Verify you ran [code]eliteautopreset[/code] at least once
[/list]
