========================================
ELITE VARIANTS - WISH COMMANDS
========================================

SPAWNING COMMANDS
-----------------
EliteVariantSpawn          - Spawn elite encounter (random tier based on zone)
UltimateVariantSpawn       - Spawn ultimate encounter (guaranteed golden)


SETTINGS COMMANDS
-----------------
EliteVariantsSettings      - Show current difficulty settings


DIFFICULTY PRESETS
------------------
Power Multiplier:
  EliteVariantsPower1.5    - Set power to 1.5x (50% stronger)
  EliteVariantsPower2.0    - Set power to 2.0x (double strength)

Level Offset:
  EliteVariantsLevel+10    - Elites spawn 10 levels above you

Ultimate Chance:
  EliteVariantsUltimate100 - 100% ultimate spawns (all golden)

Reset:
  EliteVariantsReset       - Reset all settings to defaults
                             (Power: 1.0x, Level: +0, Ultimate: 30%, Enhancements: 1.0x)


DEFAULTS
--------
Power Multiplier:      1.0x
Level Offset:          +0 (matches your level/zone)
Ultimate Chance:       30%
Enhancement Multiplier: 1.0x


NOTES
-----
- Settings persist between game sessions
- Use EliteVariantsSettings to check current configuration
- HP scales from creature's natural HP (no more damage sponges)
- Creatures keep their original factions and hostility behavior
- Weeps and Fluid Carriers are excluded from spawning
- Traders/Wardens only spawn solo or with goons
- Elite tier = White appearance, 1-3 mental mutations
- Ultimate tier = Golden appearance, 4-6 mental mutations
